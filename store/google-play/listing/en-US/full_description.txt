TRAZO plans your strength training and adapts it to the way you train.

PLANS
• Manual plan, free: build your own days and exercises, link supersets and edit the plan whenever you want.
• Automatic plan (Premium): TRAZO designs a progressive plan from your level, training days, available equipment and the muscles you want to prioritise, with blocks, deload weeks and a gentle preparation phase if you are new or coming back.
• Flexible schedule: fixed weekdays or no fixed days at all. TRAZO tells you which session comes next and lets you reschedule when life gets in the way.

TRAIN
• Log sets, weight, repetitions and RIR with fast entry, suggested loads and a rest timer.
• Swap an exercise for an alternative that fits your equipment and limitations, or add one on the fly.
• Works offline: your workouts are saved on the device and sent when you are back online.

PROGRESS
• Personal records, estimated 1RM, sets per muscle and your weekly streak.
• See the whole current block and what each week asks of you.

ADAPTATION (Premium)
• After each session tell TRAZO how it went: it adjusts loads, sets and deloads for the next ones and explains every change.

PREMIUM
Free trial included. Cancel any time from Google Play. Creating and using manual plans is always free.
